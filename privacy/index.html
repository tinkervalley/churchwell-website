Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Church life is personal. Here’s how Churchwell handles information when you use our apps, web platform and website.

Who we are

Churchwell provides software for participating churches and their members. For information a church adds to its directory, groups, forms and ministry records, that church decides the purposes of use, who has access and how long it needs the records. Churchwell processes that information to provide and support the service. Churchwell is responsible for its own website, service operations and support correspondence.

Our privacy contact is hello@churchwell.ca. Please tell us which church your question concerns.

Information we handle

  • Directory and account information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, profile photos, addresses, church mailbox numbers, sign-in access, roles and permissions. Your church may add a directory record before you first use the app.
  • Family and ministry information: household membership, parent/guardian links, child status, optional school grade, group membership, volunteer involvement, attendance and check-in records.
  • Content you or your church provide: messages, group notes, uploaded materials, form answers and attachments, announcements, and fundraiser or cause records. Form answers may contain additional personal or sensitive information depending on what your church asks.
  • Service and device information: account identifiers, authentication/session information, notification preferences and device push tokens if notifications are enabled. Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request times, browser/device information and operational logs for delivery, reliability and security.
  • Support information: the contact details and content you send when you ask for help or request a beta invitation.

Church membership and participation can reveal religious beliefs. Family, attendance, private messages and some form answers can also be sensitive. Churches should collect only what they need, explain their purpose and obtain appropriate permission.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate members, connect them with their church, provide directories and groups, deliver messages and notifications, manage forms and check-in, display church content, provide support, prevent abuse and maintain the service. Churchwell does not sell personal information or use church records for targeted advertising.

Who can see it

Access depends on church settings, directory visibility and assigned permissions. Authorized church administrators and ministry leaders can access records needed for their work. Other members may see information shared with the directory or their groups. Family visibility controls do not remove a person from a ministry’s authorized check-in roster.

Messages and form submissions are available to their intended recipients and authorized reviewers as the feature requires. A public form or public church image can be accessed through its shared link. Do not include private information in public materials. Churchwell personnel may access information when needed to operate the service, resolve a support request, investigate abuse or meet legal obligations; we do not promise end-to-end encryption.

Children and families

Children can have church directory and ministry records without signing in. A parent, guardian or authorized church staff member supplies and manages those records. Parents can manage visibility through the supported family tools; authorized ministry staff may still need information for check-in and attendance. Churchwell does not independently solicit children to register. Contact your church or our privacy contact to correct or remove a child’s information.

Providers and international processing

We use service providers to run Churchwell: Supabase for database and authentication, Cloudflare for web delivery and uploaded files, Apple for iOS distribution and push notifications, and GitHub Pages for the public website. Email delivery uses a configured email/SMTP provider. Providers process information needed to perform those functions.

Data may be stored or processed outside your province or country, including in the United States and other locations used by those providers, where local laws may permit government access. Contact us for questions about current processing arrangements.

Video and podcast content may be delivered by YouTube, Podbean or another provider selected by your church. When you play or open that content, the provider may receive your IP address, device/request information and other data under its own policy. See Google’s privacy policy and Podbean’s privacy policy.

Local storage, cookies and permissions

The app and web platform retain sign-in/session information and may cache photos or content to improve loading. The website does not include advertising analytics or a marketing form database; invitation links open your email client. Hosting and embedded media providers may use their own essential or media-related technologies.

You can control notifications in the app and your device settings. Photos or files you select are uploaded only for the feature you use. Churchwell does not need access to your full contacts list or precise location to provide its core features.

Retention, access and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, support the church’s documented recordkeeping needs, resolve disputes and satisfy applicable obligations. Retention varies by record type and church. Deleted information may remain temporarily in restricted backups or operational logs until their normal retention cycles complete.

To request access, correction, export or deletion, contact your church administrator or hello@churchwell.ca. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the church that controls its records. We will explain any information that must be retained and why. Signing out or disabling sign-in is not deletion. Hiding a directory entry changes visibility, rather than deleting church records.

Security

We use transport encryption, access controls and permission-scoped file access to protect information. Downloaded content and time-limited file links may remain available for a period after access changes. No system can guarantee absolute security. Tell us promptly if you believe an account or record has been accessed improperly.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version; material changes will be communicated through an appropriate service notice. Questions, concerns or requests can be sent to hello@churchwell.ca.

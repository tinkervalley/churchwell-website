Email support ↗

Start with your church

Your church manages directory entries, family membership, ministry permissions and sign-in eligibility. For those changes, your church administrator is usually the best first contact. Churchwell can help with technical problems or route a privacy request.

How do I log in?

Your church must be using Churchwell and have your email in its directory with sign-in enabled. Choose Log in, enter that email and use the code sent to your inbox. There’s no separate member sign-up or password.

I haven’t received a sign-in code.

Check junk/spam, confirm the email with church staff and request a fresh code if needed. Use the newest code. If email still does not arrive, contact support with your church name and the approximate time you tried. Never send us your code or password.

Why can’t I see my church or group?

Ask your church to confirm your directory email, sign-in access and group membership. If you belong to more than one participating church, choose the correct church in the app. Group content depends on membership and permissions.

How do I update my family or photo?

Open People and your profile or My Family. Use Add to family for an existing or new person. Member family and profile-photo requests may need staff approval before they appear. Contact staff for corrections you cannot make yourself.

Can I hide my family’s directory entries?

Use the available directory visibility settings or ask church staff. Parents can control their children’s visibility. Ministry check-in access and administrative records are separate from the public member directory.

How do I manage notifications or unwanted messages?

Use notification settings in the app and your device settings. In Chats, use the conversation’s available block or report actions for unwanted contact. Contact church staff or Churchwell for further help. Churchwell is not an emergency service.

Can I use Churchwell in a browser?

Yes. Visit app.churchwell.ca and use the email-code login. The web app works on desktop and mobile.

How do I request deletion of my account or data?

Email hello@churchwell.ca with your church name and the email used to sign in. We’ll verify the request and coordinate account access and church-held records with the appropriate administrator. Tell us whether your request concerns one church or all Churchwell access. Some records may need to be retained for stated legal or administrative reasons. Signing out alone does not delete data. Read our privacy policy for more details.

How can our church try the beta?

The current beta is free and invite-only for churches. Email us about your community to request an invitation. The planned starting subscription after beta is CAD $40/month; paid terms will be confirmed before any paid service begins. Dedicated branded apps are arranged separately.

Reporting a technical issue

Include your church name, device model, iOS/browser version, app version if available, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot can help, but hide private names, contact details, messages and children’s information first. Do not send sign-in codes, passwords, API keys or full directory exports.

Privacy or security concern?

Email hello@churchwell.ca. Please describe the issue without including someone else’s personal records or any secret credentials.

A few things,
before we begin.

How do we try Churchwell?

Email hello@churchwell.ca to request a beta invitation. We’ll talk through your church’s needs and help you get set up. The invited beta is free.

Do members need to sign up?

No separate sign-up is needed. Church staff add members to the directory and enable sign-in. Members enter that email address and receive a sign-in code.

Do we need a printer for check-in?

No. The Android kiosk can record check-ins without printing. Enable labels for the groups that need them, then connect a compatible Bluetooth thermal printer.

Can we use our existing YouTube or podcast feed?

Yes. Connect your YouTube channel or playlist and your podcast RSS feed in church settings. Your content stays with its existing provider, while members can find video, audio and series in Churchwell.

Can I belong to more than one church?

Yes. If your email has access to more than one participating church, you can choose which church to open.

Do we need a branded app?

No. The shared Churchwell app uses your church’s branding too. A dedicated app adds your name and logo to the store listing and app icon and is arranged separately.

Can we use it without installing an app?

Yes. Churchwell’s responsive web app works on desktop and mobile browsers at app.churchwell.ca.

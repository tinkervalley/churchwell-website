A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Made for your church.
Simple for your people.

How does a church get started?

Email hello@churchwell.ca to request an invitation. We’ll arrange a walkthrough and discuss the setup and app options that fit your church.

Do members need to create an account?

Members whose directory entry allows sign-in enter their email and receive a code. There’s no separate member sign-up flow.

Can members belong to more than one church?

Yes. In the shared Churchwell app, members with access to multiple churches can choose the church they want to view.

Do we need a dedicated branded app?

No. The shared Churchwell app and responsive web app use the branding configured for your church. A dedicated church app adds your name and logo to the app itself.

Can we use it without installing an app?

Yes. The web workspace is responsive and works on desktop and mobile browsers.
